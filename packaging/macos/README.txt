Cadly — macOS build (self-contained, Apple Silicon)

Run:
  open Cadly.app                                   (GUI viewer)
  Cadly.app/Contents/MacOS/cadly sample-files/as1-ug-214.stp
  Cadly.app/Contents/MacOS/cad_import_cli sample-files/as1-ug-214.stp

Or drag Cadly.app into /Applications. STEP (.step/.stp) and IGES
(.iges/.igs) files can be opened from Finder via "Open With".

The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized: on first launch macOS may show
"Cadly.app can't be opened". Right-click the app, choose Open, and confirm
— or allow it under System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Requires macOS 12 or later.
